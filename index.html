Open questions this flow raises — deliberately unresolved at this stage:

  • Screen 3 puts the heaviest friction in the flow (identity verification) before the booking, while screen 5 defers the lightest (payment) until after. Should verification move after the booking too?
  • Screen 2 shows a neutral fare comparison that labels the cheapest option. Valuable to the traveller, uncomfortable for the partners paying to appear on it.
  • Screen 5 is the highest-attention moment of the session — two minutes of waiting on a live map. The discovery upsell may belong here rather than on screen 7.

Concept prototype for discussion. Not affiliated with Google, Grab, Bolt or inDrive; brand names are placeholders illustrating a multi-provider surface. No real booking or payment functionality.
